Privacy Policy

App: வானிலை - Simple Weather (com.hariism07mani.weather)
Developer: Weather Forecast · Manikandan A
Contact: manikandanmcaa@gmail.com
Last updated: 22 October 2026

Simple Weather shows and speaks the weather in Tamil and English. It has no accounts, no login, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. Our only server is a small market-price relay on Cloudflare that receives just a district name (see below).

Not a government app. Simple Weather is made by a private developer. It does not represent, and is not connected to, any government body (including IMD, NDMA or the Ministry of Agriculture). Government alerts, radar pictures and market prices are copied from public official websites: sachet.ndma.gov.in, mausam.imd.gov.in, agmarknet.gov.in and data.gov.in. For official information, check those websites.

Location

  • The app uses your phone's location only to show the weather where you are. You can say no and pick a place on the map instead.
  • To get the weather, the position (rounded to about 1 km) is sent to the weather services listed below. It is not linked to you or your phone.
  • Your last place is kept only on your phone, so the weather works without internet and for the background weather alerts.

Microphone

  • Used only while you press the microphone to ask a question or to find a place by voice. Your phone's speech service (Google) turns the voice into words; the app does not record or keep any audio.
  • For finding a place, only the words you said (the place name) are sent to the place search services.

Notifications

Used to warn you about dangerous weather (heavy rain, thunder, cyclone, heat) even when the app is closed. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Data kept on your phone only

Language, settings, chosen crops and places, and the last weather are kept on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Services the app talks to

  • Open-Meteo.com – weather forecast, air quality, place search (position or place name)
  • NDMA SACHET (Government of India) – official disaster alerts
  • GDACS (UN / EU) – active cyclones
  • aviationweather.gov – weather measured at nearby airports
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim – place search when Open-Meteo finds nothing (place name only)
  • India Meteorological Department (mausam.imd.gov.in) – live rain radar pictures (no position is sent)
  • Our own server on Cloudflare (simple-weather-mandi) – crop market prices. It receives only your district name (e.g. "Salem") and passes the question to data.gov.in (AGMARKNET, Government of India). It keeps no record of who asked.
  • Google Play – to check whether a new version of the app is available

These services receive only what is needed (a rounded position or a place name) and your internet address, like any website you visit.

Children

The app is suitable for everyone and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
